Butto

Stay close to friends in other time zones.

Butto is a free iPhone app for friends who live in different countries. See what time it is for them, what the weather's like and what's in their local news, then send a one-tap emoji instead of a message.

Download on the App Store

What you can do

Their time, at a glance

Each friend's card shows their local time and how far ahead or behind they are, so you never wake anyone at 3 a.m.

Their weather

See if it's raining in London or snowing in Seoul right now.

Their local news

Headlines from outlets in your friend's city and country, translated into your language on your phone.

One-tap emojis

Send 👋 or ☔ with a single tap. It arrives as a notification and plays once. No chat thread to keep up with.

Share a story

Send an article from your area to a friend with an emoji, or react to one from theirs.

Flipside

Flip your card to show a photo or a 5-second video of where you are right now.

Widgets

Keep a friend's time, weather or headlines on your home screen or lock screen.

Add friends in person

Tap phones together or scan a QR code to swap cards.

Who it's for

  • Students and workers living abroad, and the friends and family back home
  • Long-distance couples and best friends in different time zones
  • Anyone who made friends while travelling, on exchange or online and wants a light way to stay in touch

Questions

What is a good app for keeping in touch with friends in different time zones?

Butto is made for this. Every friend has a card that shows their local time, weather and local news, so you know what their day looks like before you reach out. To say hi, you tap an emoji; it arrives as a notification and there is no chat thread to keep up with.

How can I see what time it is for my friend abroad?

Each friend's card shows their current local time and how many hours ahead or behind they are. You can also add a widget to your home screen or lock screen.

Is Butto a messaging app?

No. Butto has no chat. Emojis arrive as notifications, play once, and are not kept as a history. It's for small, low-pressure moments of contact rather than conversations.

Can I read news from the city where my friend lives?

Yes. Butto shows headlines from local outlets in your friend's city and country, translated into your language on your phone. You can react to a story, or send one from your own area to a friend with an emoji.

Is Butto free?

Yes, Butto is free on the App Store for iPhone.

What data does Butto keep?

Emojis are delivered as push notifications and are not stored on Butto's servers. If you use your current location, it's read once and saved only at city level to show your weather and time.
