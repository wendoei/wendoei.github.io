ChangeHero

Get confident with cash, one level at a time.

ChangeHero is a free iPhone and iPad game for practising how to pay with real coins and notes and work out the right change, in British pounds, euros, US dollars, Korean won and Japanese yen.

Download on the App Store

What you can do

Real coins and notes

Five currencies: British pound, euro, US dollar, Korean won and Japanese yen. You pay from a wallet of that country's actual coins and notes.

Pay the right amount

Tap coins and notes to pay for groceries, coffee and bakery items, first one item at a time, then two together.

Give the right change

Switch sides and work out the change for one item, two items and bigger baskets.

Eight levels per currency

Each currency has eight levels that get harder step by step, including a coins-only round and two timed rounds (45 and 30 seconds). You have three hearts per round.

Currency guide

Look at every coin and note front and back, with details about each one, plus fun facts that end in a quick quiz.

Badges and Game Center

Earn a badge when you master a currency, and unlock Game Center achievements.

Progress in iCloud

Your level progress is saved with iCloud, so it can follow you between your iPhone and iPad.

Who it's for

  • Travellers who want to get used to a currency before a trip
  • People moving, studying or working abroad who will pay with cash in a new country
  • New cashiers and shop staff practising how to give change
  • Students practising everyday money maths

Questions

How can I practise paying with a foreign currency before a trip?

ChangeHero lets you practise with the real coins and notes of the country you are visiting. You pay for everyday items from a wallet of that currency, then move on to working out the change, so the money already feels familiar when you arrive.

Which currencies does ChangeHero have?

Five: the British pound, the euro, the US dollar, the Korean won and the Japanese yen.

What do the levels in ChangeHero teach?

Each currency has eight levels. Levels 1 to 4 are about paying: single items, two items added together, paying with coins only, and a 45-second timed round. Levels 5 to 8 are about change: change for one item, for two items, for bigger baskets, and a 30-second timed round.

Is ChangeHero free?

Yes, ChangeHero is free to download. All eight levels are free in the first currency you choose. In other currencies the first level is free, and a one-time in-app purchase unlocks every currency pack. The app shows ads, which a separate one-time purchase removes.

Does ChangeHero work on iPad?

Yes. ChangeHero runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and your level progress is saved with iCloud.

Can I see what each coin and note looks like in ChangeHero?

Yes. The currency guide shows every coin and note front and back, with details about each one and fun facts that end in a quick quiz.
