Jihye Kim · Apps

Small apps for staying close, reading slowly, and handling money abroad.

I'm Jihye Kim, an independent iOS developer. Each app does one thing and tries to do it quietly and well.

ButtoSee your friends' local time, weather and news in other countries, and send a one-tap emoji instead of a message.
LettrdVincent van Gogh's 907 letters, delivered one at a time at the pace he wrote them.
ChangeHeroA free iPhone and iPad game for practising how to pay with real coins and notes and work out the right change, in British pounds, euros, US dollars, Korean won and Japanese yen.
NiftyConverterA simple currency converter with up-to-date exchange rates.
All apps are for iPhone and free on the App Store.
