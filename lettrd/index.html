Lettrd

Van Gogh's letters, one at a time.

Lettrd is a free iPhone app that delivers Vincent van Gogh's 907 letters one at a time, in English or Korean, at the pace he actually wrote them. A letter arrives, you read it, and then you wait — the way Theo did, not knowing what comes next.

Download on the App Store

What you can do

All 907 letters

Every surviving letter Van Gogh wrote, from a restless young art dealer in London to the wheatfields of Auvers.

Delivered, not listed

One letter at a time. The wait between them is proportional to the silence Van Gogh himself left before writing again, and your iPhone tells you when the next one lands.

English or Korean

Translated from the public-domain Dutch and French originals, not taken from a copyrighted scholarly edition.

Next to the original

Show his original Dutch or French beside the translation — or English beside Korean — and switch as you read.

The art he describes

More than 2,200 images sit beside the text: his own paintings and drawings, the works by other artists he writes about, and the sketches he drew inside the letters.

Who he is talking about

Tap an underlined name for a short card on that person, place, term or sum of money — often with a portrait and one of their works, and a link out to Wikidata, Wikipedia or Maps.

His journey

A visual timeline of his life, period by period, showing how far through it your reading has taken you.

Underline, note, keep

Mark the sentences that stay with you, write a private note on them, label the letters you archive, and save the paintings you love.

Each letter is translated and annotated by hand before it is sent, so letters open up as that work is finished. If you reach the last one that is ready, delivery resumes on its own — there is nothing to do and nothing to buy.

Who it's for

  • Korean readers who would rather follow the correspondence in Korean

Questions

Is there an app to read Vincent van Gogh's letters?

Lettrd is a free iPhone app built for exactly this. It holds all 907 of Van Gogh's surviving letters and delivers them one at a time rather than as a list you scroll, with the paintings and sketches he describes shown beside the text.

Can I read Van Gogh's letters in Korean?

Yes. Lettrd reads in English or Korean, and follows your iPhone's language. The translations were made from the public-domain Dutch and French originals rather than taken from a copyrighted scholarly edition, and you can show the original text beside the translation, or English beside Korean.

Why does Lettrd give me one letter at a time, and can I read faster?

Because the waiting is the point. Van Gogh's correspondence ran for eighteen years, and reading it as one long archive flattens it. In Lettrd a letter arrives, you read it, and then you wait — for a few hours or a few days, in proportion to the silence Van Gogh himself left before writing again. You can choose among four delivery paces in Settings, from one close to the real intervals to a faster one, and you can pause delivery whenever you like.

Can I see the paintings and sketches Van Gogh describes in his letters?

Yes. When a letter mentions a work, the painting or the sketch he enclosed is shown next to the passage, so you can look at what he was describing as you read it. The app draws on more than 2,200 images: Van Gogh's own paintings and drawings, the works by other artists he writes about, and the sketches he drew inside the letters themselves. There is also a timeline of his life by period, from the Netherlands through Paris and Arles to Auvers.

Is Lettrd free? Does it have ads or in-app purchases?

Lettrd is free on the App Store, with no in-app purchases listed and no advertising in the app. It needs an iPhone running iOS 18 or later and is about 9 MB to download.

Does Lettrd need an account, and where do my notes go?

There is no account and no sign-up. The sentences you underline, the notes and labels you write and your reading progress are kept on your iPhone and synced between your own devices through your private iCloud database — they are not sent to Lettrd's servers. The app does send anonymous usage events, such as the device's country and which letter was opened, with no account or device identifier attached. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent.

편지에 나오는 인물·지명·용어에는 밑줄과 ⓘ 표시가 붙어, 탭하면 짧은 설명 카드가 열려요. 그림은 2,200장이 넘습니다 — 고흐 본인의 작품, 그가 편지에서 이야기하는 다른 화가들의 작품, 그리고 편지 안에 직접 그린 스케치까지.

마음에 남은 문장에 밑줄을 긋고 메모를 남길 수 있어요. 밑줄·메모·읽은 진도는 Lettrd 서버가 아니라 본인 iCloud 계정을 통해 기기끼리만 동기화됩니다. 가입도, 로그인도 없습니다. 보내지는 익명 통계가 무엇인지는 개인정보 처리방침에 적어 두었어요.

반 고흐의 편지를 읽어보고 싶었지만 분량에 밀렸던 분, 피드 말고 하루 한 편 조용히 읽을 것을 찾는 분, 그림 뒤의 글을 보고 싶은 미술사 전공자와 전시 관람객에게 좋아요. iOS 18 이상 아이폰에서 쓸 수 있습니다.
