Lettrd

Van Gogh's letters, one at a time.

Lettrd delivers the complete correspondence of Vincent van Gogh, 907 letters, one at a time and paced to the rhythm he actually wrote them. A letter arrives and you read it the way Theo once did: not knowing what comes next.

Download on the App Store

Features

The full letters

All 907 letters in clear English translation.

At his pace

Delivered gradually, at the natural pace of his life. No bingeing, no feed.

Paintings beside the words

See the paintings and sketches he describes, right next to the letter.

His journey

A visual timeline of his life, period by period, from London to Auvers.

Your notes stay yours

Underline lines and keep private notes that never leave your device.

Your collection

Save the letters and paintings you love.

Who it's for

  • Art lovers and anyone curious about Van Gogh's life
  • People who want a slow, quiet reading habit instead of another feed
  • Students of art history and literature

Questions

Is there an app to read Van Gogh's letters?

Yes. Lettrd delivers all 907 of Vincent van Gogh's letters in English, one at a time, with the paintings and sketches he mentions shown beside the text.

Do I need an account?

No. Lettrd has no account and no feed, and your notes stay on your device.

Is Lettrd free?

Yes, Lettrd is free on the App Store for iPhone.
