Lettrd Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Lettrd is made by Jihye Kim, an independent developer. It is designed so that there is as little of you in it as possible: there is no account, no sign-up, and the app never asks for your name, your email address or any other personal identifier.

What stays on your iPhone

The following are stored on your device and are not sent to Lettrd's servers:

  • Your delivery schedule and which letters have arrived
  • Which letters you have read, archived or bookmarked
  • The sentences you underline, and any note you write on them
  • The labels you give to letters you archive
  • The paintings you mark as liked
  • Your settings: delivery pace, reading mode, reading language and notification choice

iCloud sync

So that your reading carries over to your other devices, the items above are mirrored to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. That copy belongs to your Apple Account; the developer cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing is synced and everything simply stays on the device.

Letters and images

The letters and the artwork images are not bundled into the app — your iPhone fetches them from Lettrd's server and image CDN as you read, and caches them locally. Like any request to any website, those requests carry your device's IP address and are handled as part of serving the content. They do not carry an account or a user identifier, because there isn't one.

Anonymous usage events

To understand which letters and paintings people actually read and like, the app sends a small batch of anonymous events to Lettrd's own server. An event contains only:

  • what happened — a letter was opened or viewed, a sentence was underlined, un-underlined or copied, a painting was liked, letters were archived, the delivery pace was changed
  • which letter, text block and sentence position it concerned, and which painting
  • for an underlined or copied sentence, the text of that sentence of Van Gogh's, so that the record survives a later re-translation
  • the reading language, the app version, and the country of your device's regional settings

There is no user account, no device identifier and no advertising identifier attached, so events cannot be tied together into a profile or traced back to you. Your own writing is never included: notes and labels are never sent. Precise location is never collected — the country comes from your device's region setting, not from GPS. Events are queued on the device and sent in batches, so they also work offline. They are kept for up to one year.

Aggregate statistics

Lettrd also uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count aggregate figures such as new users, active users and retention. Instead of a device identifier it uses a salted hash that changes every day, so the same iPhone is not recognisable from one day to the next.

Notifications

The notification telling you a letter has arrived is scheduled on your own device. It is not a push message from a server, and granting notification permission does not give anyone a token to reach you.

No advertising

The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not use the App Tracking Transparency identifier, and nothing about your reading is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Content

Van Gogh's letters are in the public domain; the English and Korean translations were made for this app from those public-domain originals. The artwork images come from public-domain and museum sources and are shown for cultural and educational purposes.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. The mirrored copy can be deleted from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings, under your Apple Account → iCloud. Because the anonymous events carry no identifier, there is no personal record of you on the server to look up, correct or delete.

Children

Lettrd is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
