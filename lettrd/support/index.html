Lettrd support

Lettrd is made by one person. Email me and you will reach the developer directly, not a helpdesk.

Email: wendoei57@gmail.com — in English or Korean. I usually reply within a few days. A bug report is most useful with your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version from Settings → Version.

Common questions

No new letter has arrived. Is it broken?

Probably not. Letters are meant to keep you waiting: on the default pace the next one arrives anywhere from about eight hours to two days later. Settings shows your delivery status. If it says you have reached the last letter that is ready, that is the honest answer — each letter is translated and annotated by hand before it is sent, and delivery resumes by itself once the next ones are finished. You do not need to reinstall anything, and there is nothing to buy.

Can I make the letters arrive faster, or stop them for a while?

Yes. Settings → Delivery Speed offers four paces, from one close to the real intervals between his letters to a noticeably faster one. You can also pause delivery entirely and resume it later, and turn arrival notifications on or off.

I got a new iPhone. Will my letters and notes come with me?

Yes, as long as both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account. Your reading progress, highlights, notes, labels and liked paintings are mirrored to your own private iCloud database and are pulled down when the app opens on the new device — your journey continues where it stood, and the intro is skipped. There is no account to log in to. If Settings shows "No iCloud account", sign in to iCloud and open the app again.

Can I read in Korean, or see the original language?

The letters can be read in English or Korean, and the app follows your iPhone's language setting. In Settings → Reading Mode you can show his original Dutch or French beside the translation, show English beside Korean, or read the translation alone.

The letters or images will not load.

The text and the artwork images are fetched from the internet as you read, so the first load of a letter needs a connection; after that it is cached on the device. If a letter fails to load, check your connection and reopen the app. If it keeps failing, email me — it may be my server rather than your phone, and I would want to know.

How do I delete everything the app has stored?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. The mirrored copy can be deleted from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings, under your Apple Account → iCloud. There is no account to close and no personal record on the server — see the privacy policy.

Something in a letter looks wrong — a translation, a date, a painting.

Please tell me. The translations and the annotations are reviewed by hand, letter by letter, and reports of a mistranslation, a wrong attribution or a misplaced artwork are genuinely useful. Mention the letter number shown at the top of the letter.
